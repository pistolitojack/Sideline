===============================================================================
PART 1 — WHAT THE APP ACTUALLY DOES
===============================================================================

THE ONE-LINE VERSION
A coach films training on their phone, uploads the raw clips, and gets back
finished social-media reels — cut, captioned, and written — without touching an
editing app.

THE FULL LOOP, AS A COACH EXPERIENCES IT

1. SIGN UP + ONBOARDING (once, ~2 minutes, on a phone)
   Email sign-in (a code, no password). Then six screens that build what we call
   the Coach DNA:
     - Instagram handle (optional) — a background job later scrapes the profile
       into a text summary of their existing brand
     - Name, the sport they coach, the tones they want (Direct / Encouraging /
       No-nonsense / Funny / Technical), and a brand colour
     - A 60-second voice memo, transcribed, used so the writing sounds like them
     - Who their content is for, and what the business goal is right now
       (more private clients / fill the fall program / build the brand / sell
       online coaching)
     - Their city

2. UPLOAD (30 seconds of the coach's time)
   Pick up to 6 videos from the camera roll. Optionally tap a starting point
   ("Hype reel", "Teaching breakdown", "Motivational story",
   "Behind-the-scenes", "Client win") which fills an editable text box, or type
   a request in plain English, or send nothing at all and let the AI decide.
   Then they put the phone away.

3. THE AI DOES THE WORK (3-8 minutes, unattended)
   Six stages run on a background worker:
     - INGEST: reads each video's length and dimensions, extracts the audio
     - DIRECTOR: looks at sampled frames from every clip plus the coach's
       profile, their history, and their request — then writes a plan: which
       clips group together, what pieces to make, what each one is for, and a
       shot-by-shot recipe for each
     - TRANSCRIBE: speech to text (Deepgram)
     - UNDERSTAND: per clip, looks at frames + transcript and finds the usable
       "moments" with timestamps and a reason each one matters
     - COMPOSE: per piece, picks the exact in/out points from those moments,
       and writes the hook, caption, hashtags, call-to-action and the on-screen
       caption beats
     - RENDER: ffmpeg cuts the segments, applies transitions, crops to vertical
       9:16, burns the captions in the coach's brand colour, normalises audio,
       and produces an mp4 plus a poster frame

4. REVIEW (1-2 minutes)
   Finished reels appear as a swipe deck. Swipe right to keep, left to reject.
   Rejecting asks why (Weak hook / Wrong energy / Boring cut / Off-brand /
   Athlete issue) plus an optional note. Tapping a reel opens the detail, where
   they can ASK FOR CHANGES in plain English — "let the jump finish before you
   cut" — and the AI re-cuts it and sends it back.

5. DOWNLOAD AND POST (manual)
   Approved reels go to a "Today" tab with their caption ready to copy. The
   coach downloads the video to their camera roll and posts it themselves.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT FROM A TEMPLATE TOOL
The AI learns the individual coach. Every rejection, every typed note, every
"ask for changes" is stored and fed back into the planning stage next time. We
have one measured example: a coach rejected a reel with the note "the same clip
played twice", and the next session's shortest shot went from 2.0 seconds to
5.5 seconds — no code or rule was changed, only that sentence travelling through
the system. After each session the AI also writes itself a note about what it
learned about that coach.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT IN IT
  - No posting to Instagram/TikTok. The coach downloads and posts manually.
  - No scheduling or calendar.
  - No music or licensed audio. Reels keep the original clip audio.
  - No team accounts, no multi-user, no client management.
  - No analytics on how posts performed after they go out.
  - NO PAYMENT SYSTEM AT ALL. Nothing charges anybody. There is no Stripe, no
    subscription, no trial logic, no plan limits tied to money.

CURRENT LIMITS BUILT INTO THE PRODUCT
  - 3 upload sessions per coach per rolling 24 hours (enforced in the database)
  - 6 videos per session, 200MB per video
  - Reels capped at 60 seconds
  - Raw footage deleted after 30 days; finished reels kept indefinitely

HOW FINISHED IT IS, HONESTLY
It works end to end and I use it weekly on real footage. The cutting is
decent-to-good and improving; roughly one reel in three has something I'd fix.
There is one user — me. Nothing has been tested with another human being, so
every claim about what coaches want, including mine, is a guess.

PART 2 — WHAT IT COSTS TO RUN
===============================================================================

--- A. VARIABLE COST: AI, PER UPLOAD SESSION -------------------------------

This is the number I'm most confident in. It's calculated from the actual code
— the real frame-sampling logic, the real token budgets — at Anthropic's
published rate for claude-sonnet-4-6 ($3 per million input tokens, $15 per
million output).

A session sends the AI sampled still frames from each video plus text. One
frame costs about 621 tokens.

  SMALL   3 clips (7.5s, 5.5s, 17.1s), 3 reels out — a real session of mine
          34 frames · 36k input + 11.2k output tokens  ........... $0.28

  TYPICAL 3 clips x 20s, 4 reels out
          54 frames · 51k input + 12.7k output tokens  ........... $0.34

  HEAVY   6 clips x 30s, 5 reels out
          144 frames · 113k input + 18.4k output tokens  ......... $0.62

  Each "ask for changes" re-cut  ............................... +$0.05

  Working figure: ~$0.35 per session, ~$0.40 including one revision.

  PER COACH PER MONTH:
     4 sessions/mo   API $1.59   storage +0.63GB   egress 0.94GB

--- D. WHERE THE CEILINGS ARE ----------------------------------------------

  Supabase Pro includes 100GB storage and 250GB egress. At 12 sessions per
  coach per month that's roughly:
      ~53 coaches on storage — AND THIS SHRINKS EVERY MONTH, because finished
        reels are kept forever and only raw footage is purged
      ~88 coaches on bandwidth
  Beyond that it's $0.021/GB-month storage and $0.09/GB egress — real money but
  not frightening.

  The storage number is the one to watch. Nothing currently deletes old reels.
  That's a design decision nobody has made yet, and it's a cost decision too.

PART 4 — WHAT I'M ASKING YOU
===============================================================================

1. IS THERE A BUSINESS HERE AT ALL? Be blunt. If the honest answer is "this is
   a great personal tool and a bad business", I want to hear that now rather
   than after I spend six months on it.

2. WHAT SHOULD I CHARGE, AND HOW SHOULD IT BE STRUCTURED? Flat monthly?
   Per-session credits? Tiers? The cost is almost entirely variable and
   almost entirely AI, which I assume changes the answer compared to normal
   software.

3. WHAT'S THE RISK IN MY COST SHAPE? ~94% of my variable cost is the AI, and it
   scales linearly per session. A heavy user costs me 4x what a light one does.
   How do I price so a power user doesn't become unprofitable?

4. WHAT'S THE SINGLE BIGGEST THING MISSING BEFORE ANYONE COULD PAY ME? My
   guess is the payment system itself, but it might be something about the
   product I'm not seeing.

5. WHO IS THIS ACTUALLY FOR? I built it for private sports coaches and small
   training facilities. Is that the right market, or is the real customer
   someone else — gyms, agencies, other kinds of small business with the same
   problem?

6. WHAT WOULD YOU VALIDATE FIRST, AND HOW CHEAPLY? I have one user and zero
   evidence anyone else wants this. What's the fastest honest test?

Please be direct. If something I believe is wrong, say it's wrong and show me
why. I'd rather hear it now than find out after building on it — that exact
pattern has already cost me a month on this project once.
