===============================================================================
WHAT THE AI ACTUALLY SEES  (the question that matters most)
===============================================================================

Onboarding is not a signup form — it IS the AI's model of the coach. Below is
every field a coach fills in and which AI stages read it. Derived by reading the
prompt-building code, not guessed.

  FIELD                  STAGES THAT READ IT                           USES
  ---------------------- --------------------------------------------- ----
  sport                  director, understand, composer, revise, reflect  5
  mission                director, understand, composer, revise, reflect  5
  audience               director, understand, composer, reflect          4
  name                   composer, revise, reflect                        3
  voice_memo_transcript  composer, revise                                 2
  ig_profile (scraped)   director, composer                               2
  tones                  composer, revise                                 2
  city + state           director                                         1
  accent_hex             RENDER ONLY — the AI never sees it               0
  ig_handle              triggers the Instagram scan, nothing else        -

NOTES THAT MATTER:

  - SPORT AND MISSION reach every single AI stage. `understand` — which decides
    what counts as a usable moment in each clip — is told the sport before it
    looks at a frame. Get sport wrong and the AI is hunting for the wrong kind
    of moment before any writing happens.

  - MISSION steers the call-to-action directly: the composer is told to write a
    cta "aimed at the coach's mission". This is why mission is capped at two
    selections — "all four" gives the writer nothing to aim at.

  - THE VOICE MEMO's content is deliberately discarded. The composer is told:
    "copy STYLE only (tone, rhythm, word length), NEVER its topic or examples".
    We keep the rhythm and throw away the philosophy. It is the richest thing a
    coach gives us and we use half of it. This is an open question, not a bug —
    changing it means loosening a rule that stops copy describing a different
    exercise than the video shows, which was one of the September failures.

  - ACCENT_HEX never reaches the AI. It is passed to ffmpeg to colour the burned
    captions. That is why it keeps a default when every other field lost theirs:
    a default colour is not a false claim about who the coach is.

  - NOTHING asks what the coach SELLS, what a win looks like, or what they would
    never post. Everything collected has an output; the gap is what is never
    collected.
