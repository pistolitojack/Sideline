## 3. Planned pieces

### p1 — montage (25s, clusters c1+c2+c3)
- **Why:** Parents and kids scroll fast — this montage hits them with three
  different training zones in 25 seconds and makes the program look full,
  serious, and worth showing up to.
- **Recipe:** Hook (0-2s) fast-cut into best bag-punch impact from c1 → Beat 1
  (2-6s) wide shot of full gym establishing brand environment → Beat 2 (6-12s)
  c2 dumbbell stance drill tight on footwork → Beat 3 (12-18s) c3 ground-level
  push-up, sweaty face close-up at t=5s → Beat 4 (18-22s) smash back to c1 wide
  → Outro (22-25s) freeze frame / slow-mo on gym mural + text overlay.

### p2 — story (30s, cluster c2)
- **Why:** Parents need to see the WHY behind unconventional training — this
  piece educates them without a voiceover and positions the coach as a
  knowledgeable, results-driven trainer worth trusting with their child.
- **Recipe:** Open (0-3s) blurry motion into stance + text "This is NOT a normal
  boxing class." → Buildup (3-10s) hold on synced dumbbell stance punches →
  Context (10-18s) slow footage + callout "we build SPEED by overloading it
  first" → Payoff (18-25s) normal speed, multiple athletes, "Your kid trains
  like a pro." → CTA (25-30s) black card, gym name, city, "DM us to start."

### p3 — single (15s, cluster c3)
- **Why:** That smiling face mid-exhaustion is the most authentic, shareable
  moment in all three clips — it tells every parent and young athlete that this
  program is hard AND fun, which is exactly the brand story they need right now.
- **Recipe:** Raw and unfiltered. Start at t=5s on the smiling athlete face-down
  on the mat — that grin mid-grind is the hook. Play naturally to t=10s during
  recovery. Ambient gym sound, no music cut. Text at 5s "This face after round
  6." Text at 10s "That's the face of a champion in the making." Hard cut to
  black, no outro.

## 5. Objective observations (measurable things to re-check after Phase 3)

These are concrete, checkable patterns in the baseline — not opinions:

1. **Caption tic: "Right?" appears in ALL THREE captions.** A repeated verbal
   hook across every piece in a pack is the clearest "samey" signal we have.
   *After Phase 3, check: does one tic still repeat across every piece?*
2. **CTA convergence: all three CTAs are a DM/comment variant.** No variety in
   the ask (no "save this", "share with a parent", "come watch a session").
3. **Recipes request features that don't exist yet.** All three recipes call for
   freeze frames, slow-mo, and text overlays — Phase C features. The composer
   silently ignores them, so the *plan* is more cinematic than the *output*.
   This is a known gap, not a regression. (Logged for Phase C.)
4. **Correct guardrail behavior:** the director scored angle variety low and
   explicitly *declined* to plan a teaching piece — exactly the Phase 2 rule
   working. Keep this behavior after Phase 3.
5. **Good specificity:** p3's intent identified a genuinely specific moment (the
   grin mid-exhaustion) rather than a generic "best clip." This is the quality
   bar to beat.
6. **Hook structures:** #1 = numbers/stat, #2 = contrarian claim, #3 = observed
   detail. Reasonably varied already — Phase 3 should not regress this.
