SIDELINE — WHERE THE APP STANDS, AND WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN NEXT
A briefing for a planning conversation. Written 2026-10-06.

I'm the founder — a former coach, not a programmer. Claude Code writes the code;
I direct it, test on my phone, and judge the output. I want your honest read,
including where you think we're going wrong.

Everything factual below is read out of the code or the database, not
remembered. Where I'm guessing I've said so.

===============================================================================
1. WHAT SIDELINE IS
===============================================================================

A coach films training on their phone, uploads the raw clips, and gets back
finished vertical reels — cut, captioned, and written — without touching an
editing app. They swipe to approve or skip, can send one back with a
plain-English note, then download and post manually.

STACK
  Next.js on Vercel · Supabase (Postgres + RLS + storage) · a Node worker on
  Railway running ffmpeg · Anthropic claude-sonnet-4-6 · Deepgram for
  transcription · Apify for Instagram scraping · Resend for sign-in email.

PIPELINE (a DB-polled job table, one stage at a time)
  ingest → DIRECTOR (plans what pieces to make) → transcribe → understand
  (finds usable moments per clip) → compose (picks the cuts, writes the copy)
  → render (ffmpeg) → revise (when the coach asks for changes) → reflect
  (the AI writes itself a note about the coach) → cleanup (housekeeping)

COST
  ~$0.35 per session, ~94% of it the AI rather than infrastructure.
  $25/month Supabase Pro. Total spent building it: under $120.

USERS
  Two. Me, and my dad, who ran it for the first time on 2026-10-05.

===============================================================================
2. THE CONTEXT THAT EXPLAINS EVERY DECISION BELOW
===============================================================================

In September we stacked five changes to HOW THE AI DECIDES — thinking blocks,
motion-based frame sampling, removing the numeric pacing rules, feeding measured
"motion beats" to the editor, enforcing clip assignment in code — with no way to
measure any of them.

Output got dramatically worse. Reels with text printed over text. Two-second
reels. Copy describing a different exercise than the video showed. We reverted
the whole thing and lost a month.

Two rules came out of it and they govern everything since:
  - Never change how the AI decides without a way to measure whether it helped.
  - A pre-selected default is worse than no answer, because the AI cannot tell
    a default from a real one and reasons confidently from a lie.

===============================================================================
3. WHAT WE'VE BUILT RECENTLY  (20 commits, roughly five days)
===============================================================================

--- MEASUREMENT ---

PROMPT VERSIONS ARE NOW AUTOMATIC. Each AI stage's "version" is an 8-character
hash of the source of the function that builds its prompt. Previously a number
typed by hand, which only worked while someone remembered — and a forgotten bump
didn't blank the column, it stamped the OLD version onto a NEW prompt. It has
since caught four changes nobody would have remembered to record, including one
I made on a day I was thinking about storage.

THE SCORECARD now runs NINE automatic structural checks on every piece before
the coach sees it:
    captions_overlap · captions_run_past_end · piece_too_short ·
    segment_too_short · segment_too_long · footage_repeated · hook_too_long ·
    cut_mid_word · ends_mid_sentence
The last two were added yesterday (see §5). Every one is a yes/no fact read out
of the edit list. Pure code, no I/O, ~100 tests.

--- STORAGE (was unbounded, now has a ceiling) ---

cleanup() existed, was registered as a stage, and NOTHING EVER CALLED IT. It had
never run. 58% of the storage bucket was garbage it had been written to collect.

Before scheduling it we found two bugs that would have fired the moment it ran:
  - Reads ignored errors, so a failed query returned an empty list, nothing
    looked referenced, and EVERY REEL IN THE ACCOUNT became an orphan. Verified
    against the real planner: a failed read deletes 97 of 97 live reels.
  - Reads weren't paginated, so a truncated piece list made unseen pieces look
    unreferenced — the same failure, quieter.
A third surfaced on first run: render inserts the media row and THEN links the
piece to it, so a freshly made reel is briefly indistinguishable from an orphan.
A Railway redeploy briefly runs two workers, and an idle one would have deleted
the reel the busy one had just made.

Result: 2,266 MB → 738 MB, verified in SQL. Retention windows now cap growth:
skipped reels lose their video after 1 day (poster kept, 0.07MB vs 11.94MB),
approved after 60 days. The point isn't the megabytes — without windows there is
no steady state at all and every coach's footprint grows forever.

--- ONBOARDING (the AI was being lied to) ---

Everything was pre-selected: sport, tones, mission, colour. A coach who tapped
straight through was saved as a Direct, Encouraging Speed & Agility coach
chasing private clients, and the AI read every word as fact.

Worse, and not on anyone's list: the Coach DNA screen printed "Audience read
from your page: youth athletes & their parents" to anyone who entered an
Instagram handle. Hardcoded. Nothing read it from their page. It told the coach
a fabricated fact about themselves AND primed them to accept it two screens
later, corrupting the one field we'd just fixed.

Now: nothing pre-selected except the brand colour (which decides caption colour,
not a claim about anyone). Sport, audience and mission are multi-select. Mission
is capped at two, deliberately — every call-to-action is aimed at the mission,
and "all four" gives the writer nothing to aim at. Sport has a free-text escape
hatch, without which requiring an answer would force a lie. A missing value
reaches the AI as "?" rather than a blank or the word "null".

There is now a way back INTO onboarding from the app, with every answer
pre-filled. Previously nothing linked to it and it didn't pre-fill, so the AI's
entire model of a coach was effectively write-once.

--- RELIABILITY (all three found by the first real session) ---

NO TIMEOUT ON THE AI CALL. The SDK defaults applied — 10 minutes per attempt, 2
retries — so a request that never answers blocked the single-threaded worker for
up to 30 minutes. Now 3 minutes, ~9 worst case, ending in a real error.

NO RECOVERY FOR AN ABANDONED JOB. A job is flipped to "running" the instant it's
claimed, and nothing ever looks at "running" again. A worker that dies mid-run —
Railway redeploys on every push — orphans it forever. The coach sees "cutting
your session" until a human notices. Needed manual SQL twice in twenty minutes.
Now reclaimed automatically on boot and when idle.

SILENT STAGES. `understand` printed nothing between entering and leaving, so a
hung worker looked exactly like a working one. Now logs per clip.

--- THE INSTAGRAM BUG (the most expensive one) ---

ig_profile was NULL for both coaches while the logs said "scanned IG @handle"
every session. Two bugs behind one lie:
  - The write result was never checked. Supabase returns errors in the result
    object rather than throwing, so a failed save was invisible and the success
    line printed anyway. We paid Apify to fetch the coach's real posts, paid
    Claude to summarise them, and dropped the result — every session.
  - It also ran too late, in `compose`, which is AFTER the director. So even
    when it worked, a coach's first session was planned by a director told
    "Instagram brand: not scanned" — the one session where it knows least.

Both fixed. The scrape now runs at the top of `direct`. This is the single
biggest upgrade to what the AI knows about a coach that we've made, and it had
been silently broken the whole time.

4. WHAT THE FIRST OUTSIDE USER SHOWED US
===============================================================================

My dad, 2026-10-05. Not a programmer, never seen the app.

HE ASKED FOR THREE TEACHING VIDEOS. He got three teaching videos. He approved
two and skipped one.

His words on the two he kept: "good cutting, good prompting, good writing and a
really great caption."

HE SKIPPED THE THIRD BECAUSE A SENTENCE WAS CUT OFF MIDWAY. The scorecard had
flagged that piece for `hook_too_long` and missed the actual reason entirely.

THINGS THAT WORKED, validated on someone with no context:
  - Multi-select ("Soccer, Speed & agility")
  - City and state as two fields — he filled both. I'd typed just "GA" into the
    single field it replaced, which is what prompted the change.
  - He recorded the voice memo rather than skipping it
  - He changed the brand colour, so defaults aren't blindly accepted
  - Separate account, no contamination of my data

THINGS HE SURFACED:
  - He wanted to see the CAPTION after approving a reel. You couldn't — the
    detail sheet showed the hook and nothing else, and the caption existed only
    inside the Copy button's clipboard payload. You could copy words you had
    never read. Fixed.
  - He asked for multi-select on everything selectable. Built.
  - His audience came back as a DUPLICATE: two chips plus the same thing
    retyped as prose. My fault — multi-select made the free-text box additive
    but the placeholder still read "Or describe them yourself…". "Or" means
    instead-of. He followed the words and the words were wrong. Fixed.

WHAT IT DOESN'T TELL US: teaching works. Hype, story, behind-the-scenes and
client win have never been run end to end by anybody.

===============================================================================
5. THE THREE THINGS I THINK COME NEXT
===============================================================================

--- A. STOP THE MID-SENTENCE CUTS (half done) ---

We can now DETECT them. We have not changed how the AI cuts — deliberately,
because until yesterday there was no way to measure whether a change helped.

What's left: run a session or two to get a baseline count, change the composer's
instruction to respect speech boundaries, then compare using the prompt
fingerprint to separate before from after.

We can't measure backwards — the flags on existing pieces predate the checks,
and re-scoring needs transcripts cleanup has already deleted.

--- B. PHASE C: THE VISUAL LAYER (not started, detailed separately) ---

The honest inventory of what the renderer does today:
  ONE font (the Linux default, the only one installed in the container)
  TWO caption styles (hook on a coloured box; body with an outline), both
    static, both centred
  FIVE transitions
  The only thing that differs between two coaches is the accent colour.
  NOTHING differs between a teaching reel and a hype reel.

The AI writes differently for every coach. It does not LOOK different for any of
them. For a product pitched as "an AI employee that learns YOUR style", that's
the gap between what we claim and what we ship.

--- C. THE SCORECARD-FEEDBACK IDEA (not started) ---

Every piece records its flags. Nobody shows them to the AI. So it writes a
13-word hook, the scorecard notices, and the AI never finds out. All three of my
dad's hooks were over the 8-word limit; so were mine, consistently, for weeks.

Telling the AI its own track record is cheap and — crucially — MEASURABLE. Count
hook_too_long before, make the change, count after.

6. WHAT'S STILL BROKEN OR UNKNOWN
===============================================================================

  - Hooks are consistently too long. 3 of 3 on the last session. Known cause
    (the AI never sees its own flags), unfixed.
  - footage_repeated still fires. We fixed one cause in September (the director
    planning a reel longer than the footage supports) and it appeared twice in
    three pieces yesterday, so there is a second cause we haven't found.
  - Only ONE content format has ever been validated.
  - Review-behaviour tracking (dwell time, whether the detail view was opened)
    is silent and undisclosed. Fine while it's family; needs a privacy policy
    before a pilot. [BLOCKER FOR ANY REAL PILOT]
  - API keys that appeared in screenshots have not been regenerated. [BLOCKER]
  - I don't know whether our captions sit UNDER Instagram's own UI overlay. If
    they do, every caption we've burned has been partly covered. [CONFIRM]
  - One lint error in Today.tsx, pre-existing, unrelated to anything above.

2. ARE WE OVER-ENGINEERING THE MEASUREMENT? We now have a scorecard, prompt

   Is any of it a maintenance trap I should cut?

3. THE PATTERN I KEEP HITTING. Three separate times now the cause was the same

   success anyway — the cleanup that said it removed 217 files while removing

   it just discipline?

4. AM I READY FOR A MULTI-COACH PILOT? The learning loop is per-coach and
   demonstrably works. The visual layer is identical for everyone. Which of

   want this?
